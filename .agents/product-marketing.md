## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Your first pull-up has a date. (hero headline, `index.html`)
**What it does:** PullUp Master builds an adaptive pull-up plan from zero (dead hangs, negatives, band-assisted reps) and rewrites it after every session based on a one-tap "how did it feel?" rating, until the user reaches 1, 10, then 20+ pull-ups.
**Product category:** Fitness / strength-training coaching app, single-movement focus (pull-up to muscle-up).
**Product type:** Mobile app. iPhone + Apple Watch (App Store id6799707900) and Android (Google Play `com.pullup.master`).
**Business model:** Freemium subscription. Free: onboarding + 2 full sessions, no account needed. Pro: Annual (3-day free trial) or Monthly. Prices shown in local store currency; not stated on the site. `[NEED: actual price points per store/region if copy should mention them]`

## Target Audience
**Target users:** Adults who cannot yet do a pull-up, or can do only a few, and want a structured path with a bar at home, a park or a gym.
**Decision-maker:** The user themselves (B2C, impulse-to-considered purchase after 2 free sessions).
**Primary use case:** Getting the first clean pull-up, then building to 10+ reps, weighted and muscle-up work.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Tell me exactly what to do today at the bar, so I stop guessing.
- Make it harder only when I am ready and back off before I stall or get hurt.
- Show me a date and proof that the work is moving it.
**Use cases:**
- Zero-to-one beginner (Foundation block)
- Few reps to real volume (First reps, Volume blocks)
- Advanced / weighted / muscle-up prep
- Training from the wrist on Apple Watch with the phone on the floor

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Pull-ups are a binary skill; generic programs either assume you can already do one or give you a static spreadsheet that never adapts.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic fitness apps treat pull-ups as one exercise among hundreds, with no progression logic for the zero-rep case.
- Spreadsheets and YouTube programs do not adapt to how a session actually felt.
- Personal coaching is expensive for a single movement.
**What it costs them:** Weeks of random training, stalled progress, shoulder/elbow irritation from pushing too hard too soon.
**Emotional tension:** "The bar keeps winning." Wanting a visible, dated goal instead of vague hope.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Plan rewrites after every session from a single "Easy / OK / Hard / Failed / Pain" rating.
- Starts from true zero (Foundation block) and goes to muscle-up prep (6 blocks).
- Retest days place the user on the right block instead of guessing.
- Pain flag swaps the movement and lightens the session.
- Apple Watch app runs the whole session from the wrist, saved to Apple Health.
- Weeks-to-first-rep estimate and Sunday recap that says whether the date moved.
- No account needed to start; data stays on device unless the user opts into sync.

## Objections
| Objection | Response (from site FAQ) |
|-----------|----------|
| I can't do a single pull-up | That is exactly who the Foundation block is for. |
| What equipment do I need? | Any bar you can hang from; a band helps but every exercise has an alternative. |
| How long until my first pull-up? | Depends on start point; the app estimates weeks after onboarding and updates as you train. |
| I can already do a few | Onboarding places you on the right block so you never repeat mastered work. |
| Do I need an account? | No. Optional Email / Google / Apple sign-in only for backup and sync. |
| Is there an Android version? | Yes, on Google Play (Apple Watch features are iOS only). |
| Is this medical advice? | No. Training tool only; flag pain and consult a professional. |

**Anti-persona:** People looking for a general gym program, bodybuilding splits, or cardio tracking.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Stalled progress with a static plan or no plan.
**Pull:** A dated goal and a plan that visibly reacts to each session.
**Habit:** Existing fitness app or notebook; YouTube routines.
**Anxiety:** Another subscription; "will it work for me at zero?"; injury fear. Answered by 2 free sessions, no account, pain flag, disclaimer.

## Customer Language
**How they describe the problem:** `[NEED: verbatim quotes from store reviews, support mail or community posts]`
- Site-derived phrasing: "can't do a pull-up yet", "the bar stops winning", "stuck at 3 reps".
**Words to use:** first pull-up, dead hang, negatives, band-assisted, retest, block, plan rewrites, from zero.
**Words to avoid:** hype ("crush", "insane gains"), medical promises, guaranteed timelines, "AI-powered".
**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Block | One of six program stages (Foundation, First reps, Volume, Advanced, Weighted, Muscle-up path) |
| Retest | Test day that measures true max and places the user on a block |
| Rung | A step within the climb; onboarding places the user on the right rung |
| Streak freeze | One missed week forgiven without losing the weekly streak |
