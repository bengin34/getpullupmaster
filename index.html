FAQ

Do I need an account to use PullUpMaster?

Yes. An account (Email, Google, or Apple) is required so your workout log and program progress can be securely saved and synced to your devices.

Does PullUpMaster count reps automatically?

No. You log your reps and sets manually after each set — there is no camera or motion-based automatic counting.

What happens to my data if I switch phones?

Sign in with the same account on your new device and your full program progress and workout history sync automatically.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. You can delete your account and all associated data from inside the app, or by contacting support@qrodesk.com.

How much does PullUpMaster Pro cost?

Pro is available as a $4.99 monthly or $29.99 yearly subscription, billed through the App Store or Google Play. Restore purchases is available in-app.
