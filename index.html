After each exercise you tap how it felt. That's all the plan needs to decide your next session — so it gets harder exactly when you're ready, and backs off before you stall.

FAQ

Before you
hang.

Something else? Ask us.

Can I start if I can't do a single pull-up?

Yes — that's exactly who the Foundation block is for. You start with dead hangs, scapular pulls, slow negatives, Australian rows and band-assisted reps, which build the specific strength your first pull-up needs.

What equipment do I need?

A bar you can hang from — a doorway bar, a park, or the gym. A resistance band helps for assisted reps, and every exercise has an alternative if you don't have one.

How long until my first pull-up?

It depends on where you start. After onboarding the app estimates how many weeks your first rep is away, and the estimate updates as you train — your weekly recap tells you whether it moved.

I can already do a few. Is it still for me?

Yes. Onboarding places you on the right block — First reps, Volume, Advanced, Weighted or the Muscle-up path — so you never repeat what you've already mastered.

Do I need an account?

No. You can train without an account and your data stays on your device. Create one with Email, Google or Apple only if you want your history backed up and synced.

Which languages does the app support?

The app is available in more than 30 languages, including English, German, Turkish, Spanish and French.

Is there an Android version?

PullUp Master is available on iPhone, Apple Watch and Android (Google Play).

Is this medical advice?

No. PullUp Master is a training tool. Train within your ability, flag pain when it shows up, and see a professional if it persists.
